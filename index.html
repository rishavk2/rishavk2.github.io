About

I am an M.S. student in Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, focusing on controls and robotics. I work in the Reliable Autonomy Lab, advised by Prof. Sayan Mitra. Before that, I earned my B.S. in Electrical Engineering with a minor in Computer Science, also at Illinois.

Research

I am interested in vision-based navigation and control for agile autonomous drones. My current work looks at how high-speed drones can fly and localize themselves from onboard cameras alone, without GPS. This includes generalizable vision-based drone racing in photorealistic Gaussian-splatting simulation, and using event cameras to recover absolute position when conventional images are motion-blurred.
