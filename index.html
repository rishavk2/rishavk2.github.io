Biography

I am an M.S. student in Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, focusing on controls and robotics. I work in the Reliable Autonomy Lab, advised by Prof. Sayan Mitra, on vision-based navigation for agile autonomous drones.

Before graduate school, I earned my B.S. in Electrical Engineering with a minor in Computer Science at Illinois, where I led the data-acquisition and power-distribution teams on Formula SAE. I have also interned as a software engineer at Boeing and served as a teaching assistant for several ECE courses.

Research

I am interested in vision-based navigation and control for agile autonomous drones, and in particular how high-speed drones can localize themselves from onboard sensing alone, without GPS. My current work explores event cameras for robust localization during fast flight.
